Got it, let's play!
First you see one picture. Remember it — this is the picture to catch.
Then pictures appear one at a time in the middle of the screen.
When you see your picture, tap the screen quickly.
For any other picture, don't tap. Tapping other pictures takes points away.
Numbered circles are spread across the screen.
Tap circle 1, then circle 2. A line joins them.
Keep going in order — 3, 4, 5 — until every circle is joined.
In higher levels there is a timer. Take your time; accuracy matters most.
A stack of boxes appears for a few seconds.
Count every box, including the ones stacked behind or on top.
When the boxes disappear, enter how many you counted.
