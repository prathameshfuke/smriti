First we see a picture. Remember it — this is an image to capture.
The images then appear one by one in the middle of the screen.
When you see your photo, tap the screen quickly.
For any image, do not tap. Tapping other pictures takes points.
Numbered circles are scattered across the screen.
Tap circle 1, then circle 2. The line connects them.
Keep going in the order — 3, 4, 5 — until each circle is connected.
At higher levels there is time. Take your time; accuracy is of utmost importance.
The stack of boxes appears for a few seconds.
Count each box, including those stacked behind or on top.
When the boxes are gone, add how many you counted.
